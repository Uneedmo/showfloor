      Fair questions

      Questions brands ask us

      We already post on TikTok. Why do we need you?

      Your account builds the brand; sales at scale come from affiliates and LIVE. One account reaches a few hundred people per post. An affiliate program puts hundreds of creators — and their audiences — behind your product, paid only when they sell, and the winners become ads.

      We're not on TikTok at all. Is it too late?

      No. You start with a clean shop and no bad habits. We open and set up the shop, and affiliates create most of the content from day one.

      Will this hurt our retail stores, wholesale or Amazon?

      TikTok buyers usually discover you for the first time in the feed. Creator videos build demand that shows up everywhere — "found it at my local store" is a format we brief on purpose.

      Can our margins carry creator commission?

      We model it on your numbers first. We lead with products and bundles whose margin survives fees, commission and shipping; low-priced items ride along as add-ons.

      How do you protect our brand and claims?

      Every creator brief uses only claims you approve, with your visual rules. We review videos before they are boosted. No miracle claims, no fake reviews.

      What do you need from us?

      Access to your TikTok Shop (or permission to open one), product samples for the affiliate stage — usually 5,000–10,000 units released in waves — and stock for the LIVE studio.

      We're not in Florida.

      Neither are most creators. They work from their own homes across the country; our LIVE studio only needs product shipped to it.

      We're a manufacturer. Can this work for us?

      Yes, in two ways: we launch a TikTok-first brand on your formulas, or we bring TikTok-native brands to your private-label line. See "You make for others" above.
