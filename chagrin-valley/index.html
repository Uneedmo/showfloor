Why we love this brand

Eight bars of goat milk soap in a family kitchen in Ohio.

Ida Friedman-Kasdan, a middle-school science teacher, made her first batch in 2001 — eight bars of goat milk and oatmeal soap — while looking for something gentle for her family's skin. Twenty-five years later Chagrin Valley makes 300+ products by hand in Solon, buys oats from an Ohio farm and goat milk from a local creamery, and is run with her son Sam. Sales once quadrupled without a marketing department. That's a story creators don't need to invent — they just need to retell it.

Fair questions

Questions you may be asking

We grew without marketing for 25 years. Why change now?

Word of mouth built the brand — TikTok is word of mouth at scale. Affiliate creators are paid only when they sell, so it works like referrals, not like an ad budget.

We already post every week. Why do we need you?

Your videos are good; the reach of one account is the ceiling. An affiliate program puts hundreds of creators — and their audiences — behind your products, and the winners become ads.

How do you protect our organic claims?

Every creator brief uses only claims you approve: USDA Organic, Leaping Bunny, plastic-free, handmade in Ohio. No medical promises about eczema or any condition — we screen videos before they're boosted.

Can an $11.95 bar carry creator commission?

We lead with products and bundles whose margin survives fees and shipping — shampoo bars, deodorant, samplers and starter bundles. Half bars and lip balms ride along as add-ons.

You're in Florida. We're in Ohio.

Creators work from their own homes across the country, and our live studio only needs product shipped to it. You keep making soap in Solon — we run the shop, the creators and the broadcasts.
