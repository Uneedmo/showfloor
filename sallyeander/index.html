Fair questions

Questions you may be asking

We already have a TikTok Shop. Why do we need you?

Having a shop is not the same as having sellers. Today the account carries the whole load with its own posts. An affiliate program puts hundreds of creators — and their audiences — behind your products, paid only on sales.

Will this compete with our 2,000 stockists?

The TikTok buyer is usually someone who has never walked into one of your boutiques. Creator content also builds brand demand that shows up in stores — and the store locator stays one tap away.

Can our margin carry creator commission?

We launch only products whose margin survives fees and shipping, and lead with sets like the Best Seller Soap Set and the Mystery Soap Roll. The rest of the catalogue stays where it is.

You're in Florida. We're in New York.

Creators work from their own homes across the country, and our live studio only needs product shipped to it. You keep making soap in Beacon — we run the shop, the creators and the broadcasts.
